## 2024-05-18 - Restore focus on modal close in Preact
**Learning:** When managing focus transitions after state changes in Preact components, specifically closing a modal or unmounting an overlay, failing to trap or explicitly restore focus can cause screen reader users and keyboard navigators to lose their place on the page.
**Action:** When restoring focus to a trigger element on modal close, wrap `ref.current?.focus()` in a `setTimeout(..., 0)` to ensure the DOM has updated asynchronously. Also, use a `useRef` to track the previous state (`wasOpen`) so focus isn't accidentally stolen on initial mount.
