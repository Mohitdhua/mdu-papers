## 2026-06-30 - Focus Restoration on Modal Close
**Learning:** When building accessible modals, it's crucial to restore focus to the element that triggered the modal when it closes, ensuring keyboard users don't lose their place in the DOM. In Preact, managing focus transitions after state changes requires a `setTimeout(..., 0)` to ensure the DOM has updated asynchronously. To prevent focus from being accidentally stolen on the initial component mount due to a `[open]` effect dependency, a `useRef` flag can be used to track the previous open state.
**Action:** Always implement focus restoration when closing modals. Use a `useRef` to track `wasOpen` and `setTimeout` to handle the asynchronous nature of Preact DOM updates when managing focus.
